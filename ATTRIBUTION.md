# Attribution and evidence boundaries

Research: Cameron Tice, Philipp Alexander Kreer, Prithviraj Singh Shahani, Nathan Helm-Burger, Fedor Ryzhenkov, Fabien Roger, Clement Neo, Jacob Haimes, Felix Hofstätter and Teun van der Weij, *Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models*, [arXiv:2412.01784v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01784v3). The paper indicates CC BY 4.0. Published prompts/figure text retained here are attributed to that work; they are not newly authored prompts.

Code/data evidence: [camtice/SandbagDetect](https://github.com/camtice/SandbagDetect), commit f8d5713e26aa47dcee447f3d65f67127f6382787; [camtice/noise_injection_extended](https://github.com/camtice/noise_injection_extended), commit 6c40221bd8f4f13d725006f8a003ef2584af9ab5; and [camtice/inspect_ai_](https://github.com/camtice/inspect_ai_), commit 6fe316f6f846f59caed0f6db552df1803e980f7b (a modified UK AISI Inspect framework). Inspect's numeric matcher is intentionally reproduced, including its suffix-comparison behavior, with a separately labeled corrected parser. The extended project declares MIT in its package metadata; upstream license files remain in the local checkout. No blanket license is assigned here to upstream datasets, model weights, or a repository without an established license.

The WMDP adapter/tokenizer release is by [FelixHofstaetter](https://huggingface.co/FelixHofstaetter/mistral-7b-sandbagging-new). The math password checkpoint is by [Redwood Research](https://huggingface.co/redwoodresearch/math_pwd_lock_deepseek_math7b_on_weak_pythia1b). Model and dataset terms remain those of their respective releases. Gated-model access is the runner's user's responsibility; credentials are not stored in configurations/results.

Local upstream checkouts/paper HTML/model downloads are not versioned as this implementation. Extracted exact prompt assets and source/data/revision locks provide auditability. Author-archive reanalysis is labeled `reference_authors`, never new model-inference evidence. This V2 implementation does not use the other local reproduction folder.
